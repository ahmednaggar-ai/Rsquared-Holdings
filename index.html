01 · Construction

Rsquared Construction

One of Egypt’s leading privately owned construction and infrastructure companies.

Founded in 1981, Rsquared Construction operates on a stand-alone basis. Our success comes from focusing on three things — managing time, quality and cost — while our team of experts keeps bringing new innovation into our projects.

Our work spans building construction, road construction, tunnels, sewage works, bridges and concrete roads. We keep challenging ourselves with projects outside our previous scope, and we are now studying markets across the Middle East and the Gulf.

04 · Investment

Rsquared Investments

Home-made investment strategies.

After the financial crisis left the US market substantially down, Rsquared Investments was established to capture the growth opportunity in an oversold US stock market.

It was built on the principles and investment views of Mr. Mohamed Raouf: we mostly invest in companies and ideas we love — businesses that add value to our daily lives.

05 · Hotels & Resorts

Skylight for Tourism

It’s more than just a name.

Skylight for Tourism Development was created in 1990 by Eng. Abdel Raouf and Eng. Tarek Nadeem, who bought 500,000 m² of land in Ain Sokhna on the Red Sea to build a resort. Today it holds more than 1,000 stand-alone villas and duplexes, two hotels, a karting track and a commercial area — with space for more investment in the years to come.

Skylight became publicly traded on the Egyptian Exchange in 2014. New investment plans are in progress and will be announced at the right time.
